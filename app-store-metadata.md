### Full Description
**Silver Edge** is the ultimate companion app for coin roll hunters and silver stackers. Keep detailed records of your bank visits, track your silver coin discoveries, and analyze your hunting success rates.

**Key Features:**
🪙 **Hunt Tracking** - Record visits to different bank branches with precise location data
📊 **Detailed Statistics** - Track success rates, total coins checked, and silver found
💰 **Multi-Denomination Support** - Handle dimes, quarters, and half dollars separately
📍 **Bank Branch Mapping** - Save specific branch locations for future visits
📈 **Progress Analytics** - View all-time and weekly statistics
✏️ **Editable Records** - Update hunt results as you process your finds

**Perfect for:**
- Coin roll hunters seeking silver coins
- Collectors tracking their finds
- Anyone interested in precious metal stacking
- Hobbyists who want to optimize their hunting strategy

Whether you're a seasoned coin roll hunter or just starting your silver stacking journey, Silver Edge helps you stay organized and maximize your success rate.

**Privacy First:** All your data is stored locally on your device - no account required, no data sharing.
