# Privacy Policy for Silver Edge

**Effective Date:** [Date of App Store Submission]

## Overview

Silver Edge ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Hunt Records**: Bank names, branch locations, hunt dates, coin denominations, and silver coin findings
- **Location Data**: Bank branch addresses and locations (only when you choose to use location services)

### Information We Don't Collect
- **No Personal Information**: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal identifiers
- **No Account Data**: No user accounts or login credentials are required or collected
- **No Analytics**: We do not use analytics services that track user behavior
- **No Advertising Data**: We do not collect data for advertising purposes

## How We Use Your Information

### Local Storage Only
- All your hunt data is stored locally on your device using secure device storage
- Your data never leaves your device or gets transmitted to our servers
- We have no access to your hunt records or location data

### Location Services
- Location data is used solely to help you find and record bank branch information
- Location access is optional and can be disabled in your device settings
- Location data is not stored permanently or shared with third parties

## Data Security

- All data is stored securely on your device using iOS/Android secure storage mechanisms
- No data transmission occurs as all information remains on your device
- You maintain complete control over your data

## Data Retention and Deletion

- Your hunt data remains on your device until you choose to delete it
- You can delete individual hunts or all data through the app interface
- Uninstalling the app will remove all stored data from your device

## Third-Party Services

Silver Edge does not integrate with any third-party services that collect user data.

## Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app and on our website.
